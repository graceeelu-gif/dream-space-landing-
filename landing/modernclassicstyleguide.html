Why a guide

Why you can't just copy it from a photo

Saving inspiration is easy. Getting it to look right in your own home is the hard part.

01

Screens lie about color

A warm white on your phone can turn grey, yellow or pink on your walls. The guide gives you real paint codes, not guesses.

02

Undertones make or break it

Modern Classic works because every white, beige and brass shares the same warm undertone. One cool shade throws the whole room off.

03

Architecture does the heavy lifting

The arches and moulding are what make these rooms feel expensive. We show you how to add them, even to a plain box room.

04

Scale is everything

Pendant size, curtain height and rug size are where most rooms go wrong. The guide gives you simple numbers to follow.

05

Restraint is the secret

Fewer, better pieces. The guide shows you what to leave out, which is harder than knowing what to add.

06

One plan, every room

A consistent palette and material list means your whole home flows together instead of feeling like separate projects.
