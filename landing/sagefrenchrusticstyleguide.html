01

Screens lie about color

A warm white on your phone can turn grey, yellow or pink on your walls. The guide gives you real paint codes, not guesses.

02

Sage is tricky

Too grey and it goes cold, too yellow and it looks dated. The guide gives you greens with the right warm, earthy undertone to sit beside cream and oak.

03

Texture does the heavy lifting

Beams, plaster, rush and woven pieces are what make these rooms feel rustic. We show you how to add them, even to a plain box room.

04

Scale is everything

Pendant size, curtain height and rug size are where most rooms go wrong. The guide gives you simple numbers to follow.

05

Rustic, not themed

One too many roosters and it tips into costume. The guide shows you what to leave out, which is harder than knowing what to add.

06

One plan, every room

A consistent palette and material list means your whole home flows together instead of feeling like separate projects.
